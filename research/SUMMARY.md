# Research summary: how to start

Compiled 2026-10-26 from the seven research files in this folder. Each claim is sourced in those files. Treat anything marked ⚠ there as possibly stale.

## The short version
This can become a real side income, but plan on **low hundreds of dollars a month by month 3, not thousands.** Most clippers earn very little. The ones who earn more post a lot, in a tight niche, with strong hooks. Your edge is the pipeline: it cuts the slow part (finding and editing moments) to minutes a day, so posting volume and hook quality become the levers you control.

## Top 10 recommendations
1. **Stay on Clipping.net and add Whop Content Rewards as a second marketplace.** Clipping.net has live gaming and podcast campaigns and pays by PayPal, but only when the sponsor closes a cycle (weeks to months). Whop has the most campaigns and the clearest docs, but there are frozen-balance complaints and its Trustpilot rating was removed over fake reviews. Skip Vyro, Reach.cat and ClipAffiliates for now. *(platform-comparison.md)*
2. **Pick campaigns by effective $/1K and threshold reachability, not headline rate.** A $1/1K campaign with a 10K minimum beats a $3/1K campaign with a 500K minimum for a new account. The dashboard tracks progress toward each threshold.
3. **Post on TikTok + YouTube Shorts from day 4, add Instagram Reels in week 3, and use X only when a campaign pays for it.** Cross-posting isn't penalized; watermarks and "unoriginal" signals are. Always upload a clean export, never a file downloaded from another app. *(multi-platform-verdict.md)*
4. **Originality is the 2026 risk, more than warm-up.** TikTok, Instagram and YouTube all tightened reused-content rules in 2026. Every clip needs your own hook text, captions and framing. The pipeline does this by default. Never add another tool's watermark. *(account-ramp-up.md)*
5. **Warm up for 3 days, then post 1 a day per platform in week 1 and 2 a day in week 2.** There's a day-by-day table in account-ramp-up.md. Check each app's account-status page daily.
6. **Disclose every paid clip twice:** the platform toggle, plus "#ad" or "Paid partnership" in the video and the caption. ClipForge burns the on-screen label in and starts captions with `#ad`.
7. **Use a creator-agnostic handle**, 15 characters or fewer, letters and numbers only, never containing the clipped creator's name. Top picks: **highlightmill** (any campaign), **sitdownsnips** (podcasts/interviews), **ggcutroom** (gaming), eastoncuts, forgeclipsco. All were free on YouTube and Instagram on 2026-10-26; check TikTok and X in the apps. *(account-names.md)*
8. **Hooks decide everything:** state the point in the first 1–3 seconds, make clips 20–45 s by default, and require a payoff and standalone clarity. The selector uses a weighted rubric (hook 25%, payoff 20%, standalone 15%, emotion 15%…) with hard rejects for mid-sentence starts, sponsor reads and gambling. *(hooks-playbook.md)*
9. **Build, don't buy.** At this volume the custom pipeline costs a few dollars a month. OpusClip/Vizard/Klap cost $90–$400+ a month, and OpusClip's free tier bans commercial use. Revisit only for face-tracking reframes. *(build-vs-buy.md)*
10. **Use API keys, not your Pro plan, for automation.** Groq Whisper is free up to ~8 h of audio a day ($0.04/h after that). Claude Haiku 5.5 is about $0.004 per 1-hour source. *(api-costs.md)*

## What the APIs will cost you
Assuming each source is about 1 hour of audio:

| Volume | Groq transcription | Claude Haiku 5.5 | Claude Sonnet 5.5 (if Haiku picks poorly) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 sources/day | $0 (free tier) | ~$0.25/mo | ~$5/mo |
| 5 sources/day | $0 (free tier) | ~$0.63/mo | ~$12.60/mo |
| 10 sources/day | $0–$12/mo | ~$1.26/mo | ~$25/mo |

build-vs-buy.md quotes "$8–45/month". That figure assumes Sonnet-class selection and paid transcription. On Haiku plus the Groq free tier, expect **under $2/month**. The $15 budget cap is generous.

## Realistic earnings, months 1–3
The evidence:
- One aggregate tracker shows **~$305 lifetime per earner** at a blended ~$0.39/1K views.
- Whop's figures work out to **~$1.20 per posted video**.
- The closest real comparable, a US podcast clipper, reports **~$1,000/month** after building up.
- No "$10K+/month" claim had verifiable proof. *(success-stories.md)*

| Month | Activity | Likely views | Likely earnings | Cash in hand |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up, then 3–4 posts/day across 2 platforms, ~90 posts | Mostly a few hundred to a few thousand each; maybe 1–3 cross a 10K threshold | **$0–$50** | Often $0 (Clipping.net pays when the cycle closes) |
| 2 | 4–6/day, best formats doubled, Reels added | Median rising; a handful of 10K–100K clips | **$25–$200** | First payouts start landing |
| 3 | Settled routine, 2nd marketplace, pruned campaigns | Power law: 1–2 breakouts drive most income | **$50–$400** | Roughly tracking month 2 |

Upside exists, since one 1M-view clip on a $1/1K campaign pays ~$1,000 (if not capped), but **don't plan around it.**
